def explode_gene_symbols(df, gene_col):
    # gene symbols stored as delimited strings -> explode to rows
    # try common delimiters: ',', ';', ' '
    if gene_col not in df.columns:
        print(f"Column {gene_col} not found. Attempting to guess a gene column from columns: {df.columns}")
        # try to find a column that likely has gene symbols
        candidates = [c for c in df.columns if 'gene' in c.lower() and 'symbol' in c.lower()]
        if candidates:
            gene_col = candidates[0]
            print(f"Using guessed gene column: {gene_col}")
        else:
            raise ValueError("Could not locate a gene symbol column in dataframe.")
    out = df.copy()
    out[gene_col] = out[gene_col].fillna('')
    out['gene_symbol'] = out[gene_col].astype(str).str.replace(r'[;| ]+', ',', regex=True)
    out = out.assign(gene_symbol=out['gene_symbol'].str.split(',')).explode('gene_symbol')
    out['gene_symbol'] = out['gene_symbol'].str.strip().str.upper()
    out = out[out['gene_symbol']!='']
    return out

Pooled CRISPR screen design to identify regulators of T cell exhaustion (readout: differential TCR signaling under acute vs chronic stimulation)

A. Biological system and overall strategy
- Cell type: Primary human CD8+ (and/or CD4+) T cells purified from healthy donors. Optionally include a Jurkat reporter line for assay development, but validate in primary cells.
- Cas9 delivery: Use ribonucleoprotein (RNP) for arrayed pilot and lentiviral Cas9 for pooled screens (Cas9–T cells generated via lentiviral transduction and antibiotic selection). Alternatively use Cas9 donor line if available.
- Library: 32-gene knockout panel (3–4 sgRNAs per gene) plus controls (non-targeting, safe-targeting, positive controls such as PDCD1/CTLA4 knockouts). Total constructs ~150–200 guides.
- Readout: Flow-cytometry or reporter-based quantification of TCR signaling under:
  - Acute condition: IL-2 only (baseline proliferation/survival, minimal TCR engagement).
  - Chronic condition: plate-bound anti-CD3 (OKT3, low dose to induce persistent signaling) + IL-2 for 3–5 days to induce sustained TCR signaling/exhaustion features.
- Outcome metric: For each perturbation, compute delta signaling index = chronic condition readout − acute condition readout, then calculate the perturbation effect relative to non-targeting controls. Rank genes by the magnitude and direction of delta.

B. Assay readouts and gating
- Primary signaling readouts:
  - NFAT/NF-κB/AP-1 reporter(s) (e.g., triple-reporter or individual GFP/luciferase reporters).
  - Phospho-flow markers (p-ZAP70, p-PLCγ1, p-ERK, p-p65) at defined timepoints; or CD69/CD25 early activation markers.
- Exhaustion-associated markers (secondary readouts): PD-1, TIM-3, LAG-3, TIGIT, TOX, NR4A proteins; cytokine output (IFN-γ, IL-2) upon restimulation.
- Sorting scheme:
  - For pooled screen with reporters: Sort high- and low-reporter bins under acute and chronic conditions. Sequence integrated sgRNA to infer gene-level effects. Alternatively, do FACS-based index sorting on surface markers and reporter intensity.
  - For arrayed validation: Measure reporters and phospho-flow time courses.

C. Experimental workflow
1) Library design and production
   - 3–4 sgRNAs per gene (optimize on-target activity; minimize off-targets). Include 20–30 non-targeting controls; 5–10 safe-targeting controls; positive controls (PDCD1, CTLA4).
   - Clone into a U6-sgRNA lentiviral vector compatible with constitutive Cas9 expression (or dual-vector system).
   - Produce high-titer lentivirus; titer on T cells.

2) T cell preparation and transduction
   - Isolate CD8+ and/or CD4+ T cells (negative selection) from 3–4 donors to average donor effects.
   - Activate briefly with anti-CD3/CD28 beads (low dose) for transduction competence, or use spinfection in presence of cytokines (IL-7/IL-15) to maintain a less-differentiated state.
   - Transduce at low MOI (~0.3) to enforce single-integrant per cell; maintain library coverage ≥500–1000x per guide.
   - Select/enrich transduced cells; allow 7–10 days for editing and protein turnover.

3) Stimulation conditions and timing
   - Split edited cells into two arms, each with replicates:
     a) Acute arm: culture in IL-2 only (e.g., 50 U/ml) for the same duration as chronic arm.
     b) Chronic arm: plate-bound anti-CD3 (e.g., 1–5 μg/ml OKT3, titrated for sustained signaling without massive apoptosis) + IL-2 for 3–5 days; optionally include subthreshold anti-CD28 to model chronic costimulation.
   - For reporter screens: Measure reporter fluorescence at multiple timepoints; sort top/bottom quantiles; extract gDNA, amplify sgRNA cassettes, deep sequence.
   - For phospho-flow: Collect at defined hours-days (e.g., 1 h, 24 h, Day 3, Day 5) to capture acute vs sustained signaling differences.

4) Analysis
   - Compute guide-level enrichment scores per bin and per condition; aggregate to gene-level with MAGeCK/Robust Rank Aggregation.
   - Compute delta effect: gene_score_chronic − gene_score_acute (or ratio); rank genes by absolute delta.
   - Control QC: non-targeting distribution, replicate correlation, Gini index for library representation, editing efficiency, positive control directionality (e.g., PDCD1/CTLA4 KOs should increase signaling under chronic stimulation).

5) Validation
   - Arrayed RNP editing for top hits across donors; confirm editing (amplicon sequencing); assess reporter, phospho-flow, exhaustion markers, cytokine output, and proliferation/apoptosis.
   - Time-course RNA-seq or ATAC-seq for select hits (e.g., PTPN6, CBLB, NR4A1/2/3) to capture exhaustion programs.

D. Controls and confounders
- Include cell fitness controls; monitor viability/proliferation to avoid false positives due to cytotoxicity.
- Normalize for donor variability and ensure biological replicates.
- Avoid purely core TCR machinery in the primary panel to maximize differential effects rather than abolishing signaling.
